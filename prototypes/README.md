# Gauge prototype trial

These meshes are experimental and are not loaded by the plugin:

- `dimensional_accuracy_xy_7x5.stl` is the 7.0 x 5.0 mm XY-A challenger. Its
  control is the bundled 6.5 x 4.5 mm XY-A gauge.
- `dimensional_accuracy_zc40.stl` is the 50 mm Z-C fragment. Its 9 x 7 mm
  window provides a 40 mm working plane for comparison with Z-B.

Print comparisons in the same orientation, material, and profile. For Z,
check three ordinary 150 mm calipers. Two operators take ten Z40 readings per
model, fully removing and reinstalling the caliper for every reading. Record
failed installations as well as the readings.

A candidate passes the first gate only if every operator/caliper combination
has no failed installation and a range no greater than 0.03 mm. Z-C must also
show no systematic shift greater than 0.03 mm from Z-B before a full
40/80/120 window model is produced.

Do not replace either bundled STL based on appearance or material use alone.
